# Shared guidelines, your sales process

These guidelines describe the base. Pipeline names, stages, deadlines, cycle length
and chosen sources belong to the team using it.

1. **Review open opportunities.** Read the actual open/closed status, not an assumed
   meaning of a stage label. Keep every deal in the selected scope visible.
2. **Start with the existing plan.** Read the next step, task and scheduled meeting
   before proposing another action. An empty field alone does not prove a deal is lost.
3. **Show why.** Include the relevant facts and CRM link. Generated recommendations
   cite exact source excerpts. Facts and interpretation are distinguishable.
4. **Respect the buyer.** A refusal, postponement or incoming question requires review.
   Inactivity alone is not permission to send another message.
5. **Use the context you actually have.** Notes may come from any note taker. A contact
   belonging to the same company does not prove that their conversation concerns this
   deal. Ambiguous associations stay out of the diagnosis and remain visible as a gap.
6. **Prepare work, preserve control.** Local follow-up proposals and meeting briefs are
   reviewed by a person. No message is sent and no CRM task is created by this base.
7. **Keep the recap useful.** Group by attention needed; put detail behind each deal.
   Technical message limits split the recap, never discard deals.
8. **Choose your rhythm.** Manual by default. Schedule configuration is available;
   installation and delivery are separate extensions, not an active scheduler.
9. **Keep private data private.** Real exports stay outside source repositories. Use
   only authorized assistants and optional sources. Share fictional test cases.
10. **Improve from exceptions.** Bring back the case, the expected behavior and a test.
    Do not turn a rule from one sales cycle into a universal threshold.

## Preparations included today

- Surface an overdue recorded action and its date, without assuming it was not done.
- Bring back the latest incoming message to check its question, refusal or postponement.
- Show the next scheduled meeting alongside the recorded next step.
- With an authorized assistant, prepare a locally stored follow-up or brief with citations.
  Missing, shortened or omitted evidence can require review instead of a draft.
- Render the full review with deal links, local preparations and an export in Slack format.

- Optionally discover indirect HubSpot activity and show ambiguous links for confirmation.
- Preserve local review decisions and re-open a deal when its facts change.
- Import bilateral commitments extracted by an authorized assistant, with cited promises,
  dependencies and separate evidence for execution. Human interpretation review remains required.

Fetching Drive/Notion documents, creating mailbox drafts and delivering Slack messages
remain extension work. See [the foundations guide](community-foundations.md).
